Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how CCTV Sync ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the CCTV Sync app and related services. The information actually processed can depend on the features you choose to use.

Information we handle

When you use CCTV Sync, information may be provided by you, generated through your use of the service, or made available through permissions you grant. This may include account or profile information, settings, and information needed to provide the app's features. If you connect a Google account, the app may receive the Google account information and access you authorize during sign-in or consent.

We use information only as needed to provide and maintain the service, protect it, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

Google user data

If you use Google sign-in or connect Google services, CCTV Sync's access to Google user data is limited to the permissions you grant and the purposes presented in the app. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it. We do not share Google user data except as needed to provide the requested feature, comply with law, or protect the service and its users. We do not retain Google user data longer than needed to provide the feature, unless retention is required by law or needed for security.

CCTV Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention, and security

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-party services

The app may rely on third-party services (such as Google sign-in) that process information under their own privacy policies. Please review the applicable provider's policy for details.

Your choices

You can stop using CCTV Sync at any time. You can review or revoke third-party account access through that provider's account settings. To ask about information associated with your use of CCTV Sync, contact us using the support link below.

Children's privacy

CCTV Sync is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us through the CCTV Sync support page.
